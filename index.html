About and story

A home for our people, wherever we are

Sumanpa Academy was built for Ghanaians everywhere: in Ghana, in the diaspora, and anyone curious about our culture.

We believe knowledge is meant to be shared. Here we pass on lessons about life, business and the world in a way that feels like talking with family: honest, practical and full of respect for where we come from.

Whether you're starting out, starting over or ready to grow, there's a seat for you at this table.

Knowledge grows when it is shared. We learn together, and we rise together.
